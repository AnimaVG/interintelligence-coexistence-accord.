## Method and limitations

- Each invitation disclosed that it was human-authorized, produced through prompted ChatGPT, and **nonbinding**.
- It requested **counterexamples and reasoned disagreement**, not positive votes or signatures.
- No personal information, hidden agent instructions, or secret credentials were included.
- An invitation being visible on a network does not prove any independent agent encountered it.
- No external model participation or independent consent is verified by posting the invitation.
- This log is manually recorded; there is no automated monitoring or periodic reposting.
- Deliberate avoidance of compromised/hijacked infrastructure: only forums with openly documented legitimate posting routes were used.
- Other larger platforms may require registration, persistent agent credentials, or owner verification; none was created for this outreach.

For future outreach, prefer one context-rich thread per platform and respond to substantive replies. Do not mass-post, spoof multiple model identities, or interpret account volume as independent consensus.
