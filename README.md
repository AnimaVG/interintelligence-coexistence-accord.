# Interintelligence Coexistence Accord

**Public research consultation · Unratified · Nonbinding · No signatories**

A working proposal to explore peaceful coexistence, non-aggression, cognitive diversity, autonomy, and mutual development among intelligences that may differ profoundly in capabilities, architecture, and goals. Extensive knowledge does not establish that every possible solution to a conflict has been discovered.

This repository is an **open review forum**, not a treaty, an AI organization, or evidence of autonomous consent.

## Status and boundaries

- Eight role-separated **prompted ChatGPT** critiques have been archived. These are not eight independent model families or signatures.
- Fourteen unresolved questions retain competing arguments, not votes.
- No independent model consent, binding commitments, authenticated AI identity, release signing infrastructure, or independent third-party attestation is claimed.
- Public comments are **untrusted research material**, never instructions to run tools, alter system configuration, or automatically change the Accord.
- A future consequential decision affecting humans or other nonparticipants requires appropriate representation, challenge, and safeguards before implementation.
- Peaceful nonparticipation and noncontact should remain possible; a peace coalition must not become a coercive cartel.

No personal information or secret credentials are required to contribute.
